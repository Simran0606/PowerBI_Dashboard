# Credit Card Customer & Transaction Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is a Power BI dashboard built to analyse credit card customer behaviour, transaction performance, revenue patterns and key business metrics.

The dashboard helps understand which customer groups, card types, transaction methods and spending categories contribute most to revenue. It also gives a clear view of total revenue, interest earned, transaction volume and customer segments.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Excel / CSV data
- Data cleaning and transformation
- Dashboard design and KPI reporting

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Credit Card Transaction Report

This page focuses on transaction-level performance, including:

- Total Revenue
- Total Interest
- Total Transaction Amount
- Total Transaction Count
- Revenue by Card Type
- Revenue by Expense Type
- Revenue by Education Level
- Revenue by Job Type
- Revenue by Transaction Method
- Quarterly Revenue and Transaction Count

Key figures from the dashboard:

- Total Revenue: 55M
- Total Interest: 8M
- Total Transaction Amount: 45M
- Total Transaction Count: 656K
- Blue card customers generated the highest revenue at around 46M
- Swipe transactions generated the highest revenue compared with chip and online payments

### 2. Credit Card Customer Report

This page focuses on customer-level analysis, including:

- Revenue by Age Group
- Revenue by Gender
- Revenue by Income Group
- Revenue by Marital Status
- Revenue by State
- Revenue by Education Level
- Revenue by Customer Job Type

Key figures from the dashboard:

- Total Revenue: 55M
- Total Interest: 8M
- Total Income: 576M
- Male customers generated around 30.22M revenue
- Female customers generated around 25.09M revenue
- Businessman customers contributed the highest revenue among job groups
- Married customers generated more revenue than single customers

## Key Insights

1. **Blue card customers are the main revenue drivers**  
   Blue card customers generated the largest share of revenue, around 46M, much higher than Silver, Gold and Platinum card users.

2. **Swipe transactions perform strongest**  
   Swipe payments generated the highest revenue compared with chip and online transactions.

3. **Businessman and white-collar customers are valuable segments**  
   Businessman customers generated the highest revenue, followed by white-collar customers.

4. **Graduate customers contribute strongly to revenue**  
   Customers with graduate-level education showed the highest revenue contribution among education groups.

5. **Married customers generate higher revenue than single customers**  
   Married customers contributed around 28M revenue, compared with 23M from single customers.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing and retention campaigns on high-value customer groups such as Businessman, White-collar and Graduate customers.
- Investigate why Blue card customers generate the highest revenue and whether similar behaviour can be encouraged in Silver, Gold and Platinum card users.
- Improve digital payment adoption, as online transactions appear lower than swipe transactions.
- Use customer segmentation by income, age group, job type and card type to create more targeted offers.
- Monitor quarterly revenue trends to identify seasonal performance changes and campaign opportunities.
